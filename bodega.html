Description

Bodega is a commercial truck warehouse designed to support logistics and freight operations for the Illinois Department of Transportation (Carlile Group). The facility accommodates large-scale vehicle circulation, loading, and storage requirements, prioritizing functional efficiency and a clear-span structural layout to serve daily truck traffic.

The construction documents cover the technical detailing required for permitting and execution, translating the design intent into buildable drawings that address structural coordination, dimensional accuracy, and code compliance for an industrial-scale facility.
