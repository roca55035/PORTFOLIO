Description

Linka is a casual apparel concept designed for a compact, high-traffic retail footprint. The layout prioritizes an open, uninterrupted sales floor to maximize product visibility and customer flow, while positioning the point-of-sale near the storefront entrance for both loss-prevention control and quick checkout access.

The plan is organized into three zones: a glazed storefront vestibule that draws customers in from the street; a central open-plan sales floor housing garment racks and feature displays; and a compact back-of-house block containing two fitting rooms, a stockroom for merchandise receiving, a small management office, and a staff restroom.

This project reflects hands-on experience translating a retail brand's spatial and operational needs — circulation, sightlines, and merchandising zones — into a buildable construction-ready floor plan.
