Description

Casa 100 is a compact residential project designed to maximize spatial efficiency within a limited footprint. The layout is organized around an open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area, promoting flexibility and natural interaction between spaces. Large openings and carefully placed windows enhance natural lighting and cross ventilation, creating a comfortable indoor environment throughout the day.

The project emphasizes clean lines, functional design, and a strong connection between interior and exterior spaces. Material selection focuses on durability and low maintenance, combining contemporary finishes with a warm, residential character.
