Description

Roca Coffee is a contemporary coffee shop designed around a warm, tactile material palette and an organic architectural language. A sculpted stone bar — carved with a raw, striated finish — anchors the space, contrasting with the fluid, wave-like wood-slat ceiling above that softens the acoustics and draws the eye through the room.

The layout balances a functional service zone with a communal seating area framed by full-height glazing, allowing natural light to animate the textured plaster walls throughout the day. Pendant globe lights suspended at varying heights add a layer of warmth and intimacy, reinforcing the project's focus on creating a calm, design-forward gathering space within a commercial setting.
